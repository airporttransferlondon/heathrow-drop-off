Heathrow Airport Drop-Off Charges: What You Need to Know in 2025

Confused about the £5 drop-off fee? Learn how to pay correctly, avoid £80 fines, and discover why booking a licensed minicab from your local area (Hendon, Cricklewood, Stanmore, Peckham, and more) is the smarter, cheaper choice.

How Much Is the Heathrow Drop-Off Charge?

As of 2025, Heathrow drop off charge a mandatory £5 fee for every private vehicle that uses the designated drop-off zones outside Terminals 2, 3, 4, and 5.

This charge applies regardless of:

  • Time of day (even at 3 AM)
  • Number of passengers
  • Duration of stop (as long as you enter the zone)

The fee is enforced via **Automatic Number Plate Recognition (ANPR)** cameras. If you don’t pay within 48 hours, you’ll receive a Penalty Charge Notice (PCN) of **£80**—reduced to **£40** if paid within 14 days.

Official Drop-Off Rules at Heathrow Airport

Heathrow’s drop-off policy is strictly enforced to reduce congestion and emissions. Key rules include:

  • Only **pre-booked, licensed private hire vehicles (PHVs)** may use the free terminal forecourt drop-off areas.
  • All other private vehicles must use the **paid drop-off zones** and pay the £5 fee.
  • No waiting, circling, or double-parking is allowed—even for seconds.
  • Drop-off zones are monitored 24/7 by ANPR cameras and traffic enforcement officers.
  • Unpaid fees are escalated to the DVLA, and fines can affect future vehicle tax or MOT compliance.

Always ensure your vehicle registration is entered correctly when paying—typos can result in fines despite payment.

How to Pay the £5 Drop-Off Fee

Payment is simple and must be completed **within 48 hours** of your drop-off:

  1. Go to the official Heathrow Drop-Off Payment Portal.
  2. Enter your vehicle registration and the date/time of drop-off.
  3. Verify your journey details and pay £5 securely via debit/credit card.
  4. Save your confirmation email or screenshot as proof of payment.

Note: Third-party apps or websites cannot process this payment—only Heathrow’s official site is valid.
